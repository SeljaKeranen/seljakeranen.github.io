# AI-native ways of working

Most of my everyday building now happens with coding agents. That changes less
about the goal than people expect and more about the shape of the work: I spend
more time deciding what is worth doing and whether it actually holds, and less
time typing the parts a machine is happy to type.

The split I keep coming back to is simple. The agents are good at volume,
patience, and the tenth near-identical change. I am good at taste, at knowing
which problem is the real one, and at reading the room the model cannot see. The
work goes wrong when I hand over the second kind of thing and keep the first, or
when I forget that a reviewer with judgment is still the point.

The other thing that matters is memory. Knowledge that lives only in one
person's head gets
rediscovered on every project, at full price. So I write things down in a form
the next project, and the next agent, can actually pick up. The aim is for each
project to start further along than the last one, instead of starting over with a
better model.

Living note, and fittingly, the one I most expect to rewrite.
